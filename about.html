Who we are

Eight disciplines, one accountable team.

We are a multidisciplinary agency building digital work end to end — strategy, design, code, security, 3D, motion and words.

The name is a room number. It's where the first project got built, and it still describes how we work: everyone in one place, arguing about the work until it's right.

We're based in Abuja and work with teams anywhere. Small enough that you talk to the people doing the work, structured enough to ship on a date.
