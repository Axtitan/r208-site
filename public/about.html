The room where it all gets made.

R208 started from a simple frustration: great work kept dying in the gaps between specialists. So we removed the gaps.

Who we are

Eight disciplines, one accountable team.

We are a multidisciplinary agency building digital work end to end — strategy, design, code, security, 3D, motion and words.

The name is a room number. It's where the first project got built, and it still describes how we work: everyone in one place, arguing about the work until it's right.

We're based in Abuja and work with teams anywhere. Small enough that you talk to the people doing the work, structured enough to ship on a date.

How we operate

Three rules.

01

One room, no handoff

Design, code, security and copy sit together. Nothing gets lost in translation because there is no translation.

02

Show the work weekly

You see a live link every Friday. No black boxes, no big reveals, no surprises in week eight.

03

Build it to be handed over

Documented, tested, and operable by your team. We want you to need us less over time.
